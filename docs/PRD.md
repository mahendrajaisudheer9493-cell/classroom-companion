# Classroom Companion — One-Page PRD

Pilot: Greenfield Public School, Hyderabad · Grade 8 (8A, 8B) · 62 students · 6 subject teachers · IST · no student messages 9 pm–7 am.

## Users and problem

- **Student** (13–14, often a shared family phone): work buried in group chat; won't ask for help publicly.
- **Subject teacher** (several sections): can't tell who needs attention.
- **Coordinator** (Meera): can't see which classes are falling behind.

**Root cause: silence carries no information.** Not seen, stuck and not done look alike. Students create the signal, teachers act on it, the coordinator sees it rolled up; the teacher's problem sets the north star.

## The bet and success metrics

Every student gets a private one-tap **got it / stuck / done**; a **morning triage board** turns it into "talk to these 4 today". **Chat for moments** (receive, reply, submit, create by typing, doubts in real time); **Mini App for overviews** (triage, review, feedback, history). No student messages 9 pm–7 am IST.

**North star: known-status rate** (% of student-assignments with a clear status 24 h before the deadline): about 50% today, **80%+ by week 4**.
**Supporting:** triage opened 4 of 5 school days · blocked students answered within 1 school day · 85% linked in week 1 · 70% tap "Got it" within 24 h.
**Guardrails:** low reminder volume, no quiet-hour messages, no data leaks, few teacher edits to LLM previews.

## MVP cut and week 1 / 4 / 12 plan

**Solve deeply:** per-student status · triage board · help loop · natural-language assignments, confirmed and re-notified on change · text, photo, file or in-class submissions, no duplicates · feedback · state-aware reminders.

**Minimal:** invite-code linking (one account = one student) · selected-student targeting · strict server-side access.

**Not now:** overload prevention, sibling accounts, transfers (week 4) · parents, risk Q&A, voice notes (week 12) · SSO, native apps, payments.

**Week 1:** live in 8A and 8B; gate: 85% linked. **Week 4:** sibling accounts, transfers, overload warning; gate: 80% known status. **Week 12:** risk Q&A, parent view; decide on all of Grade 8 or a second school. **Top risk:** students not yet on Telegram; mitigate with in-class onboarding and a note to parents.
